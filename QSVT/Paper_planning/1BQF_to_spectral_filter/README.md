# Paper 1 — from one bit to any polynomial (working folder)

The follow-up to the one-bit quantum filter paper (Communications Physics, 2026). Target:
Communications Physics, about 5,000 words, six figures, Methods, supplement.

Self-contained: drop this folder into Overleaf and compile `main.tex` (or `schematic.tex`) with pdfLaTeX
(two passes; no bibliography yet). The ready-made upload is
`../1BQF_to_spectral_filter_overleaf_2026-09-18.zip`; set Overleaf's main document to `main.tex`.

- `main.tex` / `main.pdf` — the paper. Introduction, Results 1 (from hits to a filter) and
  Results 2 (the problem as a spectrum) drafted (18 Sep 2026); sections 3-5 are stubs. Every number in the text is a macro from `numbers.tex`, generated by
  `../1BQF_to_spectral_filter_corpus/13_paper_numbers.py` from the paper corpus.
- `schematic.tex` / `schematic.pdf` — the skeleton: sections, key equations, candidate figures,
  barest writing. Built 15 Sep 2026 from the thesis chapter draft.
- `figures/` — local copies of the figure files and the hand-drawn circuit TikZ
  (`circuits.tex`, `circuits_qsvt.tex`, `circuit_styles.tex`). Their sources and hashes are in
  `figures/SOURCES.md`; the data behind every figure is ledgered in
  `QSVT/Efficiency_Frontier/FIGURE_DATA_SOURCES.md`. Regenerate in the chapter folder and re-copy;
  never edit a copy in place.
- The thesis chapter draft `../qsvt_thesis_chapter_draft/` is the supporting note the paper is
  cut from.

# Audit of every paper figure's data choice — plan (2026-09-02, revised)

Purpose: decide, figure by figure, whether the data behind each plot in
`qsvt_velo_paper/main.tex` is the right data, and where it should change. George reviews the
figures and chapters one by one; this file is the checklist and the record of what is known
before that walk-through.

## The rules every figure must satisfy (George, 2026-09-02)

- **Working point.** Every curve, bar and number is read at the efficiency-first working point:
  the lowest threshold that admits the true solutions (99 % of true segments kept unless the
  figure says otherwise). The fixed absolute threshold τ = 0.35 was a bug and appears nowhere.
- **Solver names.** The QSVT is one solver; its series are labelled by polynomial degree and by
  the Hamiltonian. The other two solvers are the classical inversion and the one-bit quantum
  filter (1BQF).
- **Stated configuration.** Every figure names its acceptance (fixed 2 mrad benchmark or the
  formula acceptance: 0.42 / 3.18 / 6.31 mrad for clean / moderate / heavy), noise triple, γ, δ,
  kernel, T range and repetition count.

## What is checked for each figure

1. **Configuration fidelity** — does the figure show the configuration the text discusses?
2. **Working point** — efficiency-first for every series, said on the figure?
3. **Statistics** — how many event repetitions per point; are single-rep points marked?
4. **Claim support** — does the figure carry the number or the trend the text hangs on it?
5. **Provenance** — a ledger entry (`Efficiency_Frontier/FIGURE_DATA_SOURCES.md`) naming the
   script, the input file(s) and the commit; a hand-copied file counts as a gap.
6. **Representativeness** — single event or single repetition where a population is claimed?
7. **Economy** — does it duplicate another figure; can two be merged?

Possible decisions: keep · relabel (caption or legend only) · re-aggregate (same data, new
read-out — no new solves) · regenerate (new solves) · merge · drop.
Cost of a change: caption only · re-plot from existing files · rerun a driver on the
cached events (minutes to an hour) · a new Condor campaign.

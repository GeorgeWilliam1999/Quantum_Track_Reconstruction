**Finding:** the τ\* rule ("largest τ within 1 % rel. of pooled eff max,
T≤400") is *unstable under resampling* — denser reps resolve the contaminated-
true tail, the eff max creeps up, and τ\* dives below the 1/3 and coupled-false
attractors for classical and the comb. The per-(T,rep) wp99 working points
(nb01 §4) are stable (classical 1.44 %, comb 1.44 %, 1BQF 43.9 % at T=400) and
should carry the headline; τ\* should be reported with this instability caveat
(it independently *strengthens* the QSVT VII activation-spectrum standard).
